Draft format

  • The Association conducts an offline rookie draft before each season. Participation is optional.
  • Lottery teams use NBA franchise proxies; the NBA lottery determines the Association draft order.
  • Managers submit ranked preferences through the number matching their draft slot before selections are revealed.
