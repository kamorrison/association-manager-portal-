Methodology

Rivalry Index = matchup depth (25) + competitive balance (25) + close-game rate (20) + playoff history (20) + championship history (10). Close-game definitions come from each league's source report.

The index summarizes rivalry evidence; it does not predict the next matchup. Missing close-game evidence contributes zero rather than being estimated.
