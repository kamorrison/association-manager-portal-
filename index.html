League structure

  • 16-team dynasty-keeper hybrid league using Yahoo and 9-category head-to-head scoring.
  • Active rosters contain 12 players; weekly starting lineups contain 8 players.

2026 cap limits

  • Soft cap: $398. Hard cap: $482. Teams may never exceed the hard cap.
  • Luxury tax is 10% of each dollar above the soft cap and is due after the trade deadline if the team remains over.
